About Me

Hi! I'm Harpaavan. I am a high school student in Grade 10. I am currently attending STEM Innovation Academy High School (STEMIA), which is a STEM-focused school in Calgary, AB, Canada. At STEMIA, I like to take the opportunity to explore different fields of STEM and use the resources available to build cool projects. This has led to me having an expansive skill set, ranging from graphic design, to 3D modeling, to drone flying, and much more. I like to participate in science fairs and mathematics competitions to showcase my work and add a competitive aspect into my projects. Outside of school, I like to volunteer and work with younger kids at the library. I also enjoy biking and playing tennis to keep myself active. As I go through high school, my aim is to expand my skill set, and apply some of my skills to real-world scenarios as I start to shape out my career path.

Projects

AstroBounce

AstroBounce is an endless platformer game that I developed in Unity 2D and coded with C#. This is a space-themed game, in which there is an astronaut who bounds through the universe on cosmic asteroid platforms. The player is controlled by the space button. The objective is to get the highest score. All artwork is original and made in LibreSprite and Pixen.

Mapping Memory

In this project, I developed a program that provided a more cheaper and accessible way to measure one's memory, helping to identify and treat memory problems early. To do this, I made 4 lab-based games that test a different aspect of one's memory. I used the Muse EEG headset to record the participants' brainwaves while playing these games and generate a report based on the results.

GitHub Repository →

Ambient Mood Project

This project aims to adjust one's ambience, such as lighting and sound, to match and improve their mood. I trained a Teachable Machine model that would detect a person's mood based on body posture. This was then embedded inside an HTML website which would change color and the song playing according to the mood detected.
